# Operations

[DailyMed acquisition protocol](DAILYMED-ACQUISITION.md) is the maintained guide
to the finite collector: algorithm, prepared profile, source ownership, storage
handoff and controls. Inspect live status before reporting progress or changing a job.

[Historical acquisition notes](history/dailymed/README.md) retain earlier launch,
recovery, profiling and transport experiments. They are dated evidence, not active
settings or separate algorithms to run.

[Observed release-cycle status](OPERATIONAL-STATUS.md) covers the separate manual
update/build/selection journal. Acquisition, candidate integration, release
selection and public distribution are distinct stages.
